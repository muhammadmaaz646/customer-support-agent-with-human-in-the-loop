# Refund Policy

Refunds are issued to the original payment method and typically appear
within 5-7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected.

Items that arrive damaged, defective, or incorrect are eligible for a full
refund regardless of the 30-day return window, and the customer does not
need to ship the item back unless requested.

Refund amount can never exceed the original order total. Shipping charges
are non-refundable except when the return is due to our error (wrong item
shipped, item arrived damaged).

Refunds requested outside the return window, for reasons other than
damage/defect/our error, are not covered by policy and require manager
discretion.
