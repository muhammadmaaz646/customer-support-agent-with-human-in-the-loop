# Shipping FAQ

Standard domestic shipping takes 3-5 business days. Expedited shipping
(1-2 business days) is available at checkout for an additional fee.

We ship internationally to most countries. International orders typically
take 7-14 business days and may be subject to customs fees charged by the
destination country, which are the customer's responsibility.

Once an order ships, a tracking number is emailed automatically. Orders
can also be tracked by logging into the account used to place the order.

We do not ship to P.O. boxes for orders containing furniture or oversized
items.
