# Return Policy

Items may be returned within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund
or exchange, provided the item is unused, unworn, and in its original
packaging with tags attached.

Final sale items, gift cards, and items marked "clearance" are not eligible
for return or refund under any circumstances.

Return shipping is free for defective or incorrect items. For all other
returns, the customer is responsible for return shipping costs, which will
be deducted from the refund amount.

Items returned after the 30-day window are not eligible for a refund, but
may be accepted for store credit at our discretion on a case-by-case basis.
